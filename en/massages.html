Wellness & relaxation

Massages, in your private room

Californian, Ayurvedic, Balinese, Oriental or Prenatal: our massages are performed by a partner therapist, in the loft's private massage room, by advance booking.

The massage room is reserved for couples and mixed groups. Single men are not accepted.

Treatments offered

Californian Massage

Long, flowing, enveloping movements, designed to release muscle tension and calm the mind.

Ayurvedic Massage

Ancestral Indian technique with warm oils, to rebalance the body and energy in depth.

Balinese Massage

Firm pressure and rhythmic movements inspired by Indonesia, for deep bodily relaxation.

Oriental Massage

Cross-Asian techniques, blending gentle pressure and stretching, to release built-up stress.

Prenatal Massage

Suited to pregnant women from 3 months, to gently ease tension and heavy legs.

Tailored Massage

Composed around your needs on the day with the partner therapist, to specify when booking.

Rates

Massages must be booked at least 48h in advance, subject to the partner therapist's availability.
